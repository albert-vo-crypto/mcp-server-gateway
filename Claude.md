## Operating mode

- **Default**: Concise output, direct result, minimal safe change, focused inspection.
- **Expand only** when you request: deep dive, detailed, thorough, comprehensive, verbose, all options, full review—or when needed for correctness, safety, security, or ambiguity.
- **Task-focused**: Read and change only relevant files. Avoid unrelated refactors or broad scans.
- **Before risky actions**: Ask or state scope for destructive, privileged, expensive, or broad operations.
- **Keep instructions lean**: Store rare workflows and detailed references in on-demand docs, commands, or skills.
